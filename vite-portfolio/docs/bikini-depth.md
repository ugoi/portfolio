# Bikini Bottom depth

Researched 19 September 2026 using native web search, Brave Search and primary sources.

## What is established

[Nickelodeon's official synopsis](https://www.paramountpressexpress.com/nickelodeon/shows/spongebob-squarepants) places the town “a few fathoms” below Bikini Atoll. It does not give a numeric depth. No authoritative exact metre value was found in the checked sources. The official page is search-indexed, but direct retrieval was region-blocked.

[Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory](https://marshallislands.llnl.gov/affected-areas/bikini-atoll) describes the real atoll's central lagoon as reaching approximately 65m. The [USGS geology paper](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/submarine-geology-bikini-atoll) reports a maximum of 34 fathoms (about 62m) and shallower terraces. These measure the real lagoon, not the fictional town.

A [2023 University of Leicester undergraduate physics exercise](https://journals.le.ac.uk/index.php/pst/article/download/4533/3800/14984) assumes 50m for its calculation about Sandy's dome, based on an atoll diving source. This is an illustrative assumption, not a canonical SpongeBob measurement.

## Site interpretation

The website now ends the virtual camera dive at **50m**, labelled **RUND 50 M** in the destination title. This is our lagoon-inspired artistic approximation, not a claim that the series officially establishes 50m. It replaces the previous arbitrary kilometre descent.

World scale remains 0.25m per Three.js unit. Both the actual camera position and depth instrument use this scale. The neighbourhood's modelling origin is seven metres below the final camera, at 57m, so its foreground, homes and skyline occupy a depth range around the nominal destination; the gauge measures the camera rather than claiming to be a seabed survey.

Schools occupy fixed 6–42m habitats, rays 12–34m and jellies 14–38m. Reef outcrops and particles fit the lagoon. No creature follows the camera or gains depth-band fades. Shared depth lighting automatically retains more sunlight at the shallower destination; attenuation was not artificially re-darkened to imitate the old abyss.
